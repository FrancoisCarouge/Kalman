The output model H = ∂h/∂X of the polar coordinates, at a state.

The bearing derives from the x position by -y/r², from the y position by x/r², the quantities bearing per x or y position. The range derives from them by the components of the unit vector toward the target, x/r and y/r. Neither depends on the velocity. An angle is an arc length by its radius: the ratios of the lengths are restated as the derivatives.
