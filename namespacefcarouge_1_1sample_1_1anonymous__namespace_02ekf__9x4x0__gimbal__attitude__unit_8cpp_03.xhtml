442 const state_t errors{filter.x()};

The state transition F of the errors over a step: the propagation of the errors through the integration of the nominal values.

The bias rotates into an attitude error: ∂δθ/∂δb = -R Δt, for the attitude R. An attitude error tilts the specific force into a velocity error: ∂δv/∂δθ = -[(R Δv)×], for the velocity increment Δv of the specific force. The cross product pairs the components of different axes: the north velocity error derives from the east attitude error by the down component of R Δv, the quantity north velocity per east angle, while the component is a down velocity. Their values are equal, their quantities distinct. The value of the component is therefore restated as the derivative.
