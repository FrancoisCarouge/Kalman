The code, data, and stack sizes in bytes of one filter step: one prediction and one update, per filter configuration, update form policy, and optimization level, for version 0.5.4. Each step is linked alone, without any library, for a bare-metal Arm Cortex-M4 target with its single-precision floating-point unit, with GNU 14.2.1: -mcpu=cortex-m4 -mthumb -mfloat-abi=hard -mfpu=fpv4-sp-d16. The flash memory holds the .text, .rodata, and .data sections, the random-access memory the .data and .bss sections. The filter is statically allocated. The stack is the worst case of the step compiled as one function: a build that does not inline the library calls may use more or less. The configurations are in benchmark/footprint.

The simple form is the smallest but the least numerically robust: its estimate uncertainty may lose its symmetry and positive definiteness. The Joseph form preserves them, at a higher cost.
