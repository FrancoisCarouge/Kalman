template<typename Callable >
void fcarouge::kalman_filter::internal::assign ( Callable & callable,
const auto &... values )
constexpr

Assigns a callable characteristic from its copied initializers.

The closure type of a lambda expression with captures has a deleted copy assignment operator: such a callable is replaced in place instead. The replacement is constructed before the destruction of the assigned callable, and its non-throwing move construction never leaves the characteristic destroyed. Neither form allocates.
