return value;
}}

The derivative of the range with respect to the point: the components of the unit vector from the beacon to the point.

A component of the unit vector, an axis displacement by the range, is the quantity north/length for the north axis, while the derivative of the range by that axis is the reciprocal quantity, length/north: their values are equal, their quantities distinct. The value of the component is therefore restated as the derivative.
